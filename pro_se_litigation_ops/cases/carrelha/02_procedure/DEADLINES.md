## How the court's own calendar works — decoded and verified

The PACER Deadlines/Hearings report lists a **Submission Deadline** for each motion. That
is not the response deadline. It is the date the motion is submitted to the judge, set one
business day after the response period closes so the court does not take up a motion
before the response is in.

    submission deadline = filing date
                        + 14 days   (N.D. Ga. LR 7.1(B), response period)
                        + 3 days    (Rule 6(d), service by mail)
                        + 1 day     (submission follows the response period)
                        rolled forward off weekends (Rule 6(a)(1)(C))

**Verified against three independent data points on the report:**

Three for three. Two conclusions follow, and the second one corrects an earlier error:

1. **The Rule 6(d) mail addition is confirmed, not assumed.** The court's own calendar
   builds in the three days. That was previously the largest open variable on this sheet.
   It is now closed: the `+3` is real on every deadline here.
2. **PACER's dates are decodable and internally consistent.** The earlier sheet said
   "nothing in the rule produces PACER's September 29" and directed work to the earliest
   candidate. That was wrong. The rule produces it exactly. PACER should be *reconciled*,
   not dismissed — an unexplained PACER date means the computation is incomplete, not that
   PACER is unreliable.
