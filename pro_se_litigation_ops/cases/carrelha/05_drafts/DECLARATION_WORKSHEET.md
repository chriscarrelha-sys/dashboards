## GROUP 3 — The January 2026 ledger comparison  *(¶21 — read this one carefully)*

¶21 asks you to compare the Loan History Statement enclosed with Shellpoint's
January 16, 2026 response against Doc. 37-4 and Doc. 37-5.

**It cannot be the Doc. 37-5 copy.** That one carries a generation stamp of
**March 10, 2026** — two months *after* the January letter. So the January enclosure is a
different printing of the same report.

That makes the comparison worth real attention: **if the January copy shows a different
unapplied balance, a different ending principal, or entries the March copy doesn't, that
is a discrepancy between two of their own documents.** Put the two side by side before you
answer. If they match in substance, say so. If they don't, say exactly how.

### ⚠ Seam between this declaration and the PI motion

PI Motion Part V.E states that Christopher "works in the financial-services industry in a
capacity in which adverse credit and public-record events carry **reporting or disclosure
consequences bearing on his employment**."

If your truthful answer to ¶36 or ¶37 is that you cannot name a specific registration or a
specific obligation, **then Part V.E of the PI motion overstates and must be softened or
struck.** The declaration is the sworn document; the brief has to live inside it, not the
other way around.

This is the one place the two papers can contradict each other, and it is exactly the kind
of gap opposing counsel reads for. Resolve it before you print.
