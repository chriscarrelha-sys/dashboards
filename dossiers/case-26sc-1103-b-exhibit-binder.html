02

Assembly notes

Before you print

  • Fill the page counts. The Court uses them to find things; a blank column reads as unfinished work.
  • Reconcile the tab numbers against the exhibit letters used in the filed Motion to Strike. If the motion calls something Exhibit L, the binder tab should say so on the divider.
  • Bates-stamp Tabs 02, 05, 06 and 07 if they are not already. Pillar II is an argument about specific entries on specific pages, and it fails at the podium if you cannot point to one.
  • Mark up Tab 01 — the affidavit — in the margin, flagging each paragraph the motion moves against.

Known gaps

The Farr affidavit, the filed Motion to Strike, and the loan ledger are not in the Drive folder. They are the three documents the whole binder is built around. Everything marked “Need file” above is waiting on them.

The Supplemental Brief in support of the Motion to Strike is flagged as unconfirmed. A note in the case folder records the intent to file it before serving discovery responses, and does not record whether that happened. Confirm status on the docket — if it was filed, it becomes a tab; if not, decide whether to file.

Tab 08 depends on what discovery has actually been served and answered. If Plaintiff’s RFA responses contain an admission bearing on the payment split, that exhibit becomes as important as Tab 04 and should move up in the binder.
