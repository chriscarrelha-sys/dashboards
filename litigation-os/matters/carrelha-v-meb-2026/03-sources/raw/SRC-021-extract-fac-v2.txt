--- F.1 ORIGINATION AND OWNERSHIP ---
[¶17] "On or about July 21, 2022, Plaintiffs executed the Note and the Security
Deed in connection with the HELOC. The originating lender on the face of the Loan
Documents is Lendage, LLC ('Lendage'). MERS is designated on the face of the
Security Deed as 'nominee for Lender and Lender's successors and assigns' and as
grantee of record. The face credit limit is $149,650.00. The disclosed contract
rate is 10.750% per annum, amortized over 120 months."

[¶18] "On or about August 5, 2022, based on Plaintiffs' personal knowledge and
their contemporaneously captured Bank of America mobile-app screenshot dated
August 5, 2022 at 8:20 AM (Ex. 7), funds first appeared in Plaintiff Christopher
J. Carrelha's Bank of America account in the amount of $51,409.50 - a figure
consistent with a $50,000 cash-out cap Plaintiff recalls being disclosed in the
closing package."

[¶19] "By Assignment of Security Deed recorded February 19, 2024 in Forsyth
County Deed Book 11187, Page 717 (Ex. 5), MERS as nominee for Lendage purported
to assign the Security Deed to MEB Loan Trust VIII. The Recorded Assignment
recites, verbatim, that MEB is 'a[n] CORPORATION organized under the laws of NEW
YORK.' On information and belief, MEB is a trust (New York common-law or Delaware
statutory) and not a corporation. The recital of MEB's entity type is inaccurate
on the face of the Recorded Assignment."

[¶20] "The MERS ServicerID public lookup for MIN 1015066-5000019200-2, retrieved
on March 24, 2026 (Ex. 6), identifies the Investor of Plaintiffs' Loan as 'FREED
Mortgage Trust 2022-HE1.' The MERS ServicerID does not identify MEB Loan Trust
VIII as the investor."

[¶21] "The DBRS Morningstar Presale Report for FREED Mortgage Trust 2022-HE1
(Ex. 9) states that the notes are backed by a pool of HELOCs originated by
Lendage with a Cut-Off Date of September 30, 2022. On information and belief,
Plaintiffs' HELOC - originated by Lendage in July 2022 - falls within the FREED
origination-and-date profile disclosed on the face of that publicly available
presale report."

[¶22] "The Witt Letter (Ex. 1) states, verbatim, that 'The current owner of this
loan is MEB Loan Trust VIII Trust and their address is: Mailing Address: C/O US
Bank Trust National Assoc 1101 Centre Road, Suite 203 Wilmington, DE 19805.' That
Delaware trust-servicing address is inconsistent with the 'CORPORATION organized
under the laws of NEW YORK' recital on the face of the Recorded Assignment
(Ex. 5)."

[¶23] "The record thus contains multiple unreconciled statements of ownership
requiring reconciliation: (a) MERS ServicerID - 'FREED Mortgage Trust 2022-HE1'
(Ex. 6); (b) Recorded Assignment - 'MEB Loan Trust VIII, a[n] CORPORATION
organized under the laws of NEW YORK' (Ex. 5); and (c) Witt Letter - 'MEB Loan
Trust VIII Trust,' Delaware trust-servicing address (Ex. 1). No Defendant has
reconciled these statements notwithstanding Plaintiffs' repeated written requests
for information."

--- F.4 THE LEDGER ---
[¶26] "On or about January 16, 2026, Shellpoint produced to Plaintiffs the Ledger
as an enclosure to the Witt Letter (Ex. 1). Prior to that date, Plaintiffs did not
have access to a transaction-level servicing history for the Loan."

[¶27] "Based on Plaintiffs' review of the Ledger, the Ledger contains a
single-line entry dated July 7, 2024 designated 'New Loan.' From and after that
entry, every subsequent line on the Ledger bears a fixed Due Date of 01/01/24 - a
frozen date that does not advance with the passage of time or with subsequent
transactions. The principal balance recorded on and after the 'New Loan' entry is
$140,288.02 and does not move notwithstanding multiple subsequent tendered
payments."

[¶28] "Based on Plaintiffs' review of the Ledger, the earliest visible ledger row
reflects a Late Charge Balance of $1,145.62 associated with an August 5, 2022
entry. That balance is carried forward through subsequent monthly entries without
any separate 'Late Charge Assess' transaction line explaining its origin. Because
the first payment on the Loan was not due before the Loan was funded, a Late
Charge Balance associated with the funding entry lacks a documented basis on the
face of the Ledger and requires transaction-level reconciliation."

--- COUNT IX (Failure of Consideration) LEGAL BASIS, verbatim ---
[¶116] "Legal Basis. Under O.C.G.A. 13-3-40, '[a] consideration is essential to a
contract which the law will enforce. An executory contract without such
consideration is called nudum pactum and from it no obligation arises.' Failure of
consideration renders the contract voidable at the option of the party from whom
consideration has failed. O.C.G.A. 13-3-41. This Count challenges the underlying
HELOC obligation only; it does not challenge any Defendant's assignment of the
Security Deed and is not foreclosed by Ames, 298 Ga. 732."
