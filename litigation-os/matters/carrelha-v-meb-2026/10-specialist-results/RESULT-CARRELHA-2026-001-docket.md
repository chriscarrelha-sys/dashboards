from the two orders, which were read in full. The posture after 2026-07-31 is not

## 2. Short conclusion

Removal stands and no injunction issued. The original complaint was held an
impermissible shotgun pleading and Plaintiffs were directed to replead by
2026-08-21, with dismissal cautioned for non-compliance. That deadline passed 31
days ago and its compliance is unknown. One standing obligation runs against
Defendants regardless: at least 60 days' advance notice to Plaintiffs and to the
Court before proceeding with foreclosure.

- **Against treating the case as securely in federal court:** nothing. Remand was
  denied and the order is unambiguous. The prior-exclusive-jurisdiction and
  unanimity theories are decided in this case and are not available again at the
  district-court level.
- **Against treating the motions to dismiss as gone:** they are not gone. The
  denial was expressly "as moot … with leave to seek dismissal," in two
  alternative branches. Reading Doc. 35 as a defeat for Defendants would be wrong.
- **Against treating 2026-08-21 as a soft date:** the order paired it with a
  direction to the Clerk and an express caution about dismissal. Nothing in either
  order suggests flexibility.
- **Where I looked for contrary material and found none:** both orders were read
  in full, front to back, including footnotes; the CourtListener docket and
  docket-entries endpoints were both queried. No order extending, modifying, or
  staying any deadline appears in any available source — but the absence of such
  an order in *these* sources is weak evidence, because these sources end at
  2026-07-31.
