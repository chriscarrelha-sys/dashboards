## 0. Bottom line

**One theory in this matter is strong, documented, and survives adversarial review
from all five seats:** Shellpoint is a debt collector by its own written admission; it
boarded the loan after its own reckoning of default; it received $1,899.56 on
2025-05-17 that it **never reversed and never applied**, still showing as the ending
unapplied balance ten months later; and it charged **$8,408.74** in default and
foreclosure fees against a principal that has not moved since December 2023. Every
element of that comes from the defendants' own records.

**Three things are urgent and cheap.** Four corrections to the pleading take under an
hour, require no new fact, and remove the three cheapest points the defense has — one
of them currently costs us our best allegation. Phase 0 self-help (the client's bank
records, two recorded instruments, the published notices, the client's own NOE file)
needs no discovery and closes five blocking gaps, including the one on which Counts
I–III currently fail.

**One thing has not changed since Phase 1 and still governs everything:** nobody here
can establish what was filed, or whether it was filed by the 2026-08-21 deadline.
That deadline passed 31 days ago under an order cautioning dismissal.

**A material Phase 1 correction:** Phase 1 analysed the wrong pleading. A later,
materially better 16-count version exists and supersedes that analysis. Phase 1 had
flagged exactly this risk as HD-2; the risk materialised.
